Demo Templates

28 Nunjucks demo site templates shown to potential customers at /examples/ to illustrate what their site could look like. Most business types have two style variants; pool service and landscaping have four.

Business type Templates
Barbershop barber-classic, barber-modern
Car wash carwash-express, carwash-neighborhood
Coffee shop coffee-bold, coffee-modern
Auto detailing detailing-bold, detailing-premium
Pool service pool-bright, pool-premium, pool-simple, pool-commercial
Landscaping landscape-native, landscape-bold, landscape-family, landscape-essentials

Pet grooming pet-grooming-cozy, pet-grooming-fresh
Salon salon-bright, salon-luxe
Tattoo shop tattoo-dark, tattoo-fresh
Wine shop wine-cellar, wine-natural
Yoga studio yoga-bold, yoga-calm

Each template is a self-contained .njk file. Assets (images, previews) live in src/assets/demos/{name}/. The index.njk in this folder is the gallery/index page that lists all demos.

The pool and landscaping templates keep every business-specific detail (name, phone, towns, services, prices, reviews) in the CONFIG block at the top, so customizing one for a prospect means editing that block only. Their preview.jpg is a screenshot of the demo itself; re-take it if the hero image changes.
